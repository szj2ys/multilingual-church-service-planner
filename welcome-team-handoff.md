Guest-facing wording to adapt and have reviewed: “Today we can offer [confirmed method] for [segments]. It does not cover [gap]. [Person/role] can explain the available choices.” If nothing suitable is available, say so plainly. Do not promise that follow-up material will arrive unless someone has accepted that work and given a realistic time.

At arrival, ask which language and format the guest prefers. Offer help without insisting. Show where the relevant material or person is, and check whether the explanation was understood. Do not recruit the guest or a child to fill a missing interpreter role.

When a method fails, tell the coordinator, apply the agreed fallback, and update the welcome point immediately. Let the guest decide among real available choices; do not imply a summary provides full access.
