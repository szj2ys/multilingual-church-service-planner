# Rehearsal checklist

Use the actual room, people, equipment, and copies intended for the service. This is a preparation check, not evidence that every guest's needs are met. Work through each service-plan row with someone able to review the relevant language and format.

Service/date: ______  Plan version: ______  Coordinator: ______

## Before the run-through

- [ ] Each role has an agreed person and a way to reach them during the service.
- [ ] Speakers and interpreters have the current outline, pronunciation questions, and pause cues.
- [ ] Reviewers have checked meaning and readability; planned material changes are sent back for review.
- [ ] Required materials physically exist, have the correct version, and can be located without a web search.
- [ ] The team has permission to reproduce any chosen songs, readings, or other external material.

## Follow a guest's route

Start outside the entrance. Find the welcome point, receive the offered material, reach a seat, and follow each transition. Check readability and audibility from that seat. Rehearse spoken pauses and measure actual segment times; revise the estimate rather than rushing interpretation to fit it.

| Deliberate failure | Observe and record |
|---|---|
| Guest has no phone | Can the welcome lead offer a usable prepared format? Identify exactly what remains unsupported. |
| Wi-Fi is weak or unavailable | Test without network access. Can the team locate local files or paper? Name who switches methods and when. |
| Interpreter is absent | Remove that role from the run-through. Have the coordinator withdraw the interpretation offer and brief greeters using the agreed limitation message. |
| Reviewed material is missing | Mark the row BLOCKED. Rehearse explaining the gap; do not substitute an unchecked translation and call it equivalent. |

For each failure, record **trigger observed / decision owner / action taken / guest message / unresolved gap / next owner and deadline** in the plan or handoff. If the message itself cannot be communicated reliably, record that as a gap too.

## Release the plan

- [ ] READY rows name the reviewer, rehearsal date, usable method, and tested fallback. A tick alone is not evidence.
- [ ] BLOCKED rows have a next action and owner; their support is not advertised as available.
- [ ] The coordinator and welcome lead have the same dated handoff and know how to announce a change.

After the service, invite optional feedback about what was difficult to follow. Record the specific adjustment needed without assuming silence means success.
